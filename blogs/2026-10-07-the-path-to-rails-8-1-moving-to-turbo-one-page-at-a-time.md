When we built the DNSimple app, Rails didn't have an answer for everything a modern front end needs, so we picked the best tools available at the time and filled the gaps ourselves. That has changed. With Rails 8.1, the framework comes with a clear, well-supported way to handle interactivity, JavaScript and assets, and it matches where we want to take our design and tooling.
